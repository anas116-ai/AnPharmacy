06 Usage rules

Do

  • Keep the wordmark's proportions and letter-spacing intact.
  • Use the supplied gradient or solid ink within the magenta→coral family.
  • Give the mark clear space equal to the cap height on all sides.
  • Apply P1f (monochrome ink) for stamps, letterheads, and fax.

Don't

  • Don't stretch, skew, or condense the wordmark.
  • Don't recolor outside the brand palette (no blue/green/black-red).
  • Don't add icons, emblems, or a monogram — the word is the mark.
  • Don't rotate or set the wordmark on a busy background.
