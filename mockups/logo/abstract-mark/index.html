06 · Guardrails
Do / Don't.

✓ Do

Preserve proportions. Scale uniformly; the hexagon always frames the molecule.
Keep clear space. Pad ≥ 1 node-radius around the mark.
Stay in palette. Magenta→coral only; coral node stays coral.

✕ Don't

Don't stretch the mark — it breaks the molecule.
Don't recolor outside magenta→coral.
Don't add off-hue shadows or rotate the mark.
