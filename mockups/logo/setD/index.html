Logo Usage · Do / Don't

Applies to all three Set D concepts.

Do

  • ✓Keep all color use inside the magenta→coral family (#123F3D → #17827A → #3FA79B).
  • ✓Respect the clear-space: pad the mark by at least 25% of its height on all sides.
  • ✓Use the gradient tile for favicon / app-icon contexts smaller than 48 px.
  • ✓Switch to the monochrome (ink) version for stamps, letterheads and engraving.
  • ✓Keep the aspect ratio locked — scale uniformly.

Don't

  • ✕Don't stretch, skew or rotate the mark.
  • ✕Don't recolor outside the palette (no blues, greens or neutrals as fills).
  • ✕Don't place the mark on low-contrast or clashing backgrounds without the tile.
  • ✕Don't add drop-shadows, outlines or extra effects not in this system.
  • ✕Don't reconstruct the “A” or cross from different weights than supplied.
