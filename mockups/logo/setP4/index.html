Do / Don't

Do

  • Keep "An" in the brand gradient, "Pharmacy" in ink/white.
  • Hold the geometric sans; keep tracking tight (−0.02em).
  • Use the gradient on a divider, period, or underline rule.
  • Respect minimum clear space around the lockup.

Don't

  • Don't stretch, condense, or skew the wordmark.
  • Don't recolor outside the magenta→coral family.
  • Don't add an icon, emblem, or monogram — type is the mark.
  • Don't set the tagline in low-contrast grey on tinted panels.
