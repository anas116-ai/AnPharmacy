07

Do / Don't

Keep the mark honest to the system.

Do

  • Keep the symbol and wordmark together with clear space equal to the cap height of “A”.
  • Use the brand gradient or solid ink only — both stay in-family.
  • Invert to white inside the gradient tile for app icons.
  • Respect the proportions — scale the whole lockup as one unit.

Don't

  • Recolor outside the magenta→coral family (no blue/green).
  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the mark to “fit” a space.
  • Re-architect the crossbar back into a plain line — the pulse is the idea.
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, or glows the brief didn’t ask for.
