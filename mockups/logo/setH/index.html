Logo Guidelines

Shared Do / Don’t for the Set H marks — applies to whichever concept is selected.

✓ Do

  • ✓Keep the mark within the magenta→coral family only (#123F3D · #17827A · #3FA79B).
  • ✓Scale uniformly — the same SVG holds from 16 px favicon to 256 px hero.
  • ✓Preserve clear space: pad the mark by ≥25% of its height on every side.
  • ✓Use the gradient, dark, and light presentations as provided — they are tuned for contrast.
  • ✓Use the monochrome ink version for stamps, letterheads and engraving.

✕ Don’t

  • ✕Don’t stretch, squash or rotate the mark — always lock aspect ratio.
  • ✕Don’t recolor outside the palette (no blue, green, or neutral greys on the mark).
  • ✕Don’t add drop-shadows, outlines or glows not in the source SVG.
  • ✕Don’t place on a clashing background without the provided tile or clear space.
  • ✕Don’t reconstruct the mark in a different font/grid — keep the SVG source of truth.
