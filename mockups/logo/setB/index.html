Usage Notes — Do / Don't

Shared guardrails for every concept in Set B. These keep the mark sharp, on-brand, and legally consistent across the product and print.

✓ Do

  • Keep the mark centred in its clear-space tile with generous padding.
  • Use the gradient exactly as defined (#123F3D→#17827A→#3FA79B).
  • Use the monochrome version for stamps, invoices and letterheads.

✕ Don't

  • Stretch or squash the mark — always preserve the original proportions.
  • Recolour outside the magenta→coral family (no blue, green or grey).
  • Add outlines, drop-shadows, glows or other effects not in the system.
