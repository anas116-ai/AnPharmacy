Do / Don't

brand usage guardrails

Do KEEP

  • Keep the capsule counter intact — it's the pharmacy cue.
  • Use the mark on light, dark, or gradient — it's built for all three.
  • Protect the clearance zone around the tile (≥ 1 tile radius).
  • Render the icon at 16px+ with the gradient tile for app/favicon use.

Don't AVOID

  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the tile — keep the 1:1 geometry.
  • Recolor outside the magenta→coral family (no blue/green/slate).
  • Add drop-shadows, outlines, or extra effects to the mark itself.
  • Separate the “An” from “Pharmacy” or change the wordmark weight mix.
