07 Usage Notes Do / Don't

Do

  • Keep the wordmark intact in horizontal use — the stacked lockup (P2d) is the only approved vertical split.
  • Use the brand gradient or solid magenta for the word.
  • Respect the concept's tracking; pair with the matching font.
  • Allow generous clear space around the mark.

Don't

  • Don't stretch, skew, or condense beyond the concept setting.
  • Don't recolor outside #990066→#D4148C→#FF5A8D.
  • Don't add icons, emblems, or monograms to these wordmarks.
  • Don't apply drop shadows or outlines to the text.
