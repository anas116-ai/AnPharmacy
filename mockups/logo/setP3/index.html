Usage Rules
Keep the wordmark disciplined — it is the whole brand.

✓ Do

  • ✓Keep “An” joined to “Pharmacy” — never space or break the word.
  • ✓Use the gradient only at 135° within the magenta→coral stops.
  • ✓Respect a clear-space margin ≥ the cap-height on all sides.
  • ✓Fall back to the monochrome ink version on low-color outputs.

✕ Don't

  • ✕Don’t recolor outside the palette (no blues, greens, or neutrals-as-accent).
  • ✕Don’t stretch, condense, or skew the wordmark.
  • ✕Don’t add an icon, emblem, or monogram — the type is the mark.
  • ✕Don’t outline or apply drop-shadows that break the flat, corporate read.
