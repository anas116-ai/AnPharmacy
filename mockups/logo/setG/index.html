Do / Don't

✓ Keep

  • + Stay inside the magenta→coral palette only — no blues, greens, or greys for the mark.
  • + Preserve the shape's proportions; scale uniformly from 16px to 256px.
  • + Use the white-on-gradient or white-on-ink variants on dark/colored surfaces.
  • + Keep the capsule seam, vial drop, and ECG line crisp and legible.

✕ Avoid

  • − Don't stretch, skew, or rotate the mark outside its built-in angle.
  • − Don't recolor outside the brand gradient or add drop-shadows that muddy it.
  • − Don't separate the icon from "AnPharmacy" wordmark in final lockups.
  • − Don't outline the mark in a foreign hue (e.g. black stroke on the heart).
