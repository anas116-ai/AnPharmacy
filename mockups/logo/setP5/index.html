Do / Don't

Guardrails that keep the wordmark consistent across the product.

✓ Do

  • + Keep “An” attached to “Pharmacy” — one word, one brand.
  • + Use the magenta→coral gradient family for color versions.
  • + Preserve the font weight and tracking specified per concept.
  • + Use the monochrome (P5h) ink version on stamps & letterheads.
  • + Give the wordmark clear space on all sides.

✕ Don't

  • – Don't stretch, condense, or distort the letterforms.
  • – Don't recolor outside the magenta→coral palette (no blue/green).
  • – Don't add an icon, emblem, or monogram — typography only.
  • – Don't apply drop shadows or outlines to the wordmark.
  • – Don't break “AnPharmacy” across lines or insert spacing.
