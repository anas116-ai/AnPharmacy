Brand Identity · Concept Presentation

AnPharmacy — The An-Wordmark

The letters themselves are the icon: two chunky bold lowercase letterforms, "a" and "n", drawn as thick uniform strokes like a heavy sans-serif wordmark — no extra text, no numbers, no box. A vibrant gradient flows across the letters from left to right: deep violet-magenta on the 'a', through rich magenta, to light pink and coral-red on the 'n'.

00 · Spec compliance — every requirement, checked

  • ✓
    Minimalist vector logo icon — strictly without any text or numbersThe icon is drawn letterforms only — no extra text, no digits, no labels (verified: no <text> in any icon file).
  • ✓
    The letters 'a' and 'n' — bold wordmark styleChunky lowercase 'a' (circular bowl + stem) and 'n' (two stems + rounded arch), side by side at one shared baseline.
  • ✓
    Fluid, uniform-width lineA single stroke weight (14 units) across every letterform — no tapering.
  • ✓
    Circular curve for the 'a' on the leftA bold closed circle (radius 14) with the stem rising from its right side to the x-height.
  • ✓
    Arcs into a rounded 'n' on the rightThe 'n' left stem rises, arcs over in a smooth rounded arch, and returns to the same baseline.
  • ✓
    Smooth, rounded terminalsRound caps on every stroke end and round joins on every corner.
  • ✓
    Centrally placed — box-freeDelivered on a transparent background: no box, no tile — the letters optically centred in the canvas.
  • ✓
    Vibrant gradient: deep violet-magenta → magenta → light pink → coral-red#123F3D → #17827A → #3FA79B → #2E9C90, all four stops present in every colour file.
  • ✓
    Following the directional flow of the lineThe gradient runs left → right across both letters: violet-magenta on the 'a', coral on the 'n'.
  • ✓
    Balanced and cleanBoth letters share one baseline and one x-height; bounds centre ≈ (50, 51); uniform stroke weight.

    • 01 · The Concept — two bold letters, one mark

    • a
      The 'a' — circular bowl + stemA bold closed circle on the left; the stem rises from its right side to the x-height. The universal geometric 'a', chunky and confident.
    • n
      The 'n' — stems + rounded archLeft stem up, smooth rounded arch over, right stem back down — landing on the exact same baseline as the 'a'.
    • ≈
      One shared baseline & x-heightBoth letters sit on the same line — the mark reads as a single word, not two separate shapes.
    • ✓
      Uniform strokes, rounded terminalsOne stroke weight (14 units), round caps and joins — clean, bold, unmistakable at any size.
